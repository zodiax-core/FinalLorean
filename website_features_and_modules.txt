Website Features & Modules | Lorean

1. CUSTOMER JOURNEY & SHOPPING EXPERIENCE
------------------------------------------
- Dynamic Product Discovery: Responsive grid and list views for exploring Ayurvedic collections.
- High-Fidelity Product Pages: Detailed product narratives including ingredient origins, usage rituals, and video proofs.
- Shopping Bag & Smart Checkout: Intelligent cart management with integrated shipping/tax calculations and multi-step validation.
- Interactive Order Tracking: Real-time order status monitoring for customers via unique tracking identifiers.
- Ritual Education: Integrated brand story, "The Lorean Way," and educational sustainability content.

2. ADMINISTRATIVE & CONTENT MANAGEMENT (PORTAL)
-----------------------------------------------
- Advanced Analytics Dashboard: Real-time visual tracking of orders, revenue performance, and customer trends (powered by Recharts).
- Dynamic Product Management: Full CRUD (Create, Read, Update, Delete) capabilities for product catalogs, pricing, and inventory.
- Order Lifecycle Management: Administrative control over order fulfillment status, customer communications, and historical logs.
- Global Settings Control: Configuration for branding, social media links, and site-wide notifications.
- Media Asset Management: Native support for high-quality video proof uploads and product imagery across the storefront.

3. DATA & LOGISTICS INFRASTRUCTURE
-----------------------------------
- Realtime Database: Supabase-driven persistence ensuring zero-latency updates for order and content data.
- PDF Generation: Automated order receipt and shipping label generation for both admins and customers (via jspdf).
- QR Integration: Dynamic QR code generation for streamlined order verification and scanning.
- Automated Tax Engine: Built-in regional tax logic for precise transactional calculations.
- Integrated Notification System: Multi-channel notifications for order confirmations and shipping updates.

4. USER INTERFACE & ACCESSIBILITY
----------------------------------
- Universal Dark/Light Support: Carefully crafted theme switching ensuring brand consistency across all visual preferences.
- Adaptive Responsiveness: Mobile-first design architecture optimized for smartphone, tablet, and desktop viewports.
- Enhanced Usability: Glassmorphic UI elements, custom-sculpted scrollbars, and premium typography for optimal readability.
- Performance Architecture: Built-in lazy loading and resource hints for ultra-fast LCP (Largest Contentful Paint) times.

5. CONTENT CATEGORIES & KEY PAGES
---------------------------------
- Prime: Index (Home), Collective (All Products), Seasonal Edit (Collections).
- Functional: Checkout, Order Success, Tracking Center.
- Information: Our Story, Sustainability, Careers.
- Support: FAQ Center, Shipping & Returns, Legal (Terms/Privacy).
- Identity: Secure Customer Login, Profile Dashboard.

6. INNOVATIVE INTERACTIVE ELEMENTS
----------------------------------
- Fluid Fluid Motion: Framer-motion driven micro-interactions for every button, hover, and page transition.
- Smooth Scroll: Lenis integration for a frictionless, high-end "luxury brand" scrolling feel.
- Interactive Hero Sections: High-impact visuals with dynamic overlay controls.
- Emounted Media: Video-centric product showcasing to build trust through visual proof of efficacy.
